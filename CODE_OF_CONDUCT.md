# Devin’ Labs Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment
Devin’ Labs is a collaborative development ecosystem built on respect, clarity, and creativity.

---

## Expected Behavior

All participants should:
- Be respectful in communication
- Collaborate constructively
- Provide helpful feedback
- Respect differing opinions
- Focus on improving the ecosystem

---

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following are not allowed:
- Harassment or discrimination
- Toxic or abusive language
- Deliberate disruption of projects
- Impersonation of maintainers or owners
- Spam or irrelevant contributions

---

## Enforcement

Violations may result in:
- Warnings
- Temporary restrictions
- Removal from repositories or community spaces

---

## Scope

This applies to:
- GitHub repositories
- Discord community
- All Devin’ Labs affiliated spaces
